How to Create High-Converting Ad Creatives Using Canva

This is the exact method I use to create ads for platforms like Taboola, TikTok, and Reddit.

Step 1: Use Emotional Hooks

Examples: “You Won’t Believe This”, “Limited Time Deal”, “Everyone Is Buying This”.

Step 2: Add Product Image

Upload or use Canva stock images that look real and relatable.

Step 3: Add Trust Elements

Use badges like “Top Rated”, “Best Seller”, or “Trending Now”.

Step 4: Keep It Simple

One message, one focus. Don’t clutter the design.
