How to Create Landing Page Graphics in Canva

Want a clean, professional landing page? Canva makes it easy.

Step 1: Create a Banner

Use dimensions like 1200x600 and design a bold hero image.

Step 2: Add Icons

Search Canva Elements for icons to highlight benefits.

Step 3: Use Consistent Colors

Stick to 2–3 colors for a clean, professional look.

Step 4: Export High Quality

Download as PNG for best quality and fast loading.
